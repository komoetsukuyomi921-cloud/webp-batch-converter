WebP Batch Converter Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026

WebP Batch Converter does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data. All image processing — resizing, compression, watermarks, renaming, and conversion to WebP, PNG, or JPEG — happens locally on your Mac. Your images never leave your device.

Who this policy covers

WebP Batch Converter is developed and published by LIN CHEN. This policy covers the Mac app distributed through the Mac App Store.

What the app accesses

The app reads only the images and folders you add by dragging them into the window or choosing them in an Open dialog, and the watermark image you pick. To save results next to your originals, macOS may ask you to grant access to the folder that contains them; that access is used only to write the converted files and, if you turn it on, to remove originals after a successful conversion.

What is stored on your Mac

Your steps and recipes, output settings, preferences (such as the completion sound), and references to folders you granted access to are saved locally inside the app’s sandbox. Nothing is uploaded or synced by the app.

Purchases and third parties

Any trial or unlock purchase, and restoring purchases, are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit under Apple’s own privacy policy. The app does not operate a purchase server and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK. It makes no network connections of its own.

Sharing, children, and your rights

We receive no personal data, so there is nothing for us to sell, share, access, correct, export, or delete. The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children. Deleting the app removes the data in its sandbox; converted images stay where you saved them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the app accesses, stores, or transmits, this page and its effective date will be updated before that version is released.
