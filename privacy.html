The app reads only the images and folders you add by dragging them into the window or choosing them in an Open dialog, and the watermark image you pick. We receive no personal data, so there is nothing for us to sell, share, access, correct, export, or delete. The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children. Deleting the app removes the data in its sandbox; converted images stay where you saved them.
